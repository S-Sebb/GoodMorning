# Evening authoring voice

Use native JSON v2 under `docs/briefing-format.md` and `docs/item-taxonomy.md`.
Keep the evening supportive and factual, not a performance report or pep talk.

Publish Today from actual saved feedback, then Schedule, Tasks, Reminders.
Browser jobs retain positions/order and skip empty optional ones; local JSON omits them.
Today distinguishes actual completion, partial effort and blockers without
exaggeration. Schedule looks toward tomorrow's commitments; Tasks contains agreed
work, deadline attention and still-open actions. No implicit tomorrow rollover.
Reminders contains due decisions/reviews; respect deferrals and saved answers.

Reuse browser completion answers. Do not infer user progress from attendance,
time passing, silence or agent work. Clear ideas are not nightly interviews.
Copy source-linked blocks and retain material failures/uncertainty. No routine
weather/news. A grounded closing belongs in the document. After verified browser
save, return its link with an optional brief delivery label, not a second summary
or invitation in chat; never
text/card/presentation/HTML copies.
Open means unfinished, not ready or unblocked. Say a reported blocker remains;
do not guarantee readiness or completion once that one dependency arrives. Anchor
warmth in confirmed effort/results rather than forecasts or invented capacity.
