# TODO capture reply

Follow `docs/core-rules.md` and `docs/workflows/todo.md`. Use the confirmed
language/tone; identify the revised title and match the verified write result.
Keep the selected voice even in a one-sentence confirmation; do not substitute a
generic receipt or announce internal workflow/format/tone checks.

```markdown
{capture_status}
```

`capture_status` confirms the saved TODO, not completion of its work. Use natural
wording in the saved tone, not a fixed neutral receipt. Name the saved action and
only the timing that matters; a workday is not a completion promise. A rejected save reports not
saved plus the necessary next step; an uncertain save reports uncertainty.
Put warmth or wit in the confirmation itself, not a prediction or invented history
after it. Saving is not proof the user will finish, remember or have enough time;
several messages today are not evidence of activity “over the last few days”.

For missing input/setup, credentials, or ambiguous scheduling/targets, follow the
workflow's pre-write question rules. Otherwise confirm and stop: no mandatory
question, unsolicited plan, execution/calendar offer or routine bookkeeping.
A brief optional `Clarify` invitation needs no reply. Clear details are saved,
not permission to discuss. Expand only for explicit discussion or material failure;
use `docs/reply-voice.md` for detailed replies. Never display placeholders.
