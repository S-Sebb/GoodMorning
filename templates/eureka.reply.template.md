# Idea capture reply

Follow `docs/core-rules.md` and `docs/workflows/eureka.md`. Use the confirmed
language/tone throughout; clarity and verified status come first. Identify what
was captured without repeating long raw text or claiming the idea will work.

```markdown
{capture_status}
{next_question_or_action}
```

- `capture_status`: saved pending, confirmed/organized after verified authorized
  updates, not saved after rejection, or outcome unknown pending inspection.
  Include timestamp/project when useful, not routine bookkeeping.
- `next_question_or_action`: optional `Clarify` invitation, a required unresolved
  question, or the material next step. Omit when unnecessary; require no now/later choice.
- Ask about ambiguous scheduling/targets before saving. Otherwise save clear
  supplied details first. Details do not request discussion; capture ends after
  confirmation unless discussion was explicitly requested.
- Discussion uses remaining questions only, not repeated invitations/answered
  questions. Confirmed meaning does not authorize organization/execution.
- Show real interest only when supported. A grounded takeaway/closing is optional;
  no long framing, unnecessary headings, generic caveats, research/implementation
  promises or calendar offers. Never display placeholders. Use `docs/reply-voice.md`
  for detailed discussion, not simple capture.
