# Theme image sources

`assets/web/morning-clouds.webp` is derived from **Blue Sky with Clouds** by
Jeffrey Betts, published under **CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication**.

- Source and license: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Blue_Sky_with_Clouds.jpg
- Original: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/ab/Blue_Sky_with_Clouds.jpg
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Changes: cropped to 1600 × 1000, recolored to the site's light blue/ivory palette,
  faded into the page background at the lower edge, and encoded as WebP without metadata.

The image is bundled and served locally. Viewing a theme makes no requests to
Wikimedia or any other third party. The foreground birds and First Light landscape
are original SVG artwork in this repository.

`assets/web/morning-clouds-continuation.webp` uses the same processed photograph,
mirrored vertically and joined into a 1600 × 2000 seamless continuation. Its top
and bottom match the opening image's lower edge. Cloud and bird continuations
use different repeat lengths so the combined sky does not repeat as one short tile.
